Mutation checks (sandbox, mocked unit tests)
============================================
Method: one deliberate bug at a time was put into the Phase 2 source, the full unit-test suite was run, and the source
was restored. A mutation is KILLED when at least one test fails. A surviving mutation means a hole in the tests.

First pass: 20 mutations, 18 killed, 2 survived. Two tests were added for the survivors (stale rejection of a
reachability request; the periodic-measurement timer is really removed when switched off) and both were re-checked.
Final result: 20 of 20 killed.

KILLED  history: identical events no longer folded into one entry
KILLED  history: the "episode" never ends, so a later outage is folded into an old one
KILLED  history: no cap on the number of events
KILLED  probe: overlap guard removed (two reachability requests at once)
KILLED  probe: a stale ANSWER (camera settings changed meanwhile) is accepted
KILLED  probe: a stale FAILURE (camera settings changed meanwhile) is accepted      (needed a new test)
KILLED  probe: a cancelled request is recorded as a measurement
KILLED  probe: still probes when the browser would block it as mixed content
KILLED  probe: an old "no answer" is reported as news after the stream recovered
KILLED  probe: "host unreachable" recorded on every failure (spam)
KILLED  periodic measurement: runs while the browser tab is hidden
KILLED  periodic measurement: no minimum interval
KILLED  periodic measurement: its timer is left behind when switched off             (needed a new test)
KILLED  recovery: an interrupted recovery is forgotten
KILLED  summary: an outdated failed check still shows the power/Wi-Fi warning
KILLED  summary: "Disconnected" without saying the stream is not open on this page
KILLED  clipboard: no fallback when navigator.clipboard is missing
KILLED  redact: credentials in the camera address are kept
KILLED  redact: query string (tokens) is kept
KILLED  redact: "password=..." style pairs are kept

These show the tests notice those particular bugs. They do not prove that no other bug exists.
